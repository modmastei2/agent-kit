# Example: House

Demonstrates a small-scale structure where taste is applied selectively rather than
maximally — see SKILL.md, "Context overrides taste."

## Intent
- Type: single-family house
- Style: rustic mountain (user-specified)
- Purpose: modest dwelling, not a landmark
- Scale: small — implies restraint, not full expression of every preference
- Biome: pine-forested foothills

## Design thesis
A low timber-and-stone house that steps down its own small slope in two stages, so the
roofline — not applied ornament — is the source of character.

## LOD1 — Composition
- Primary mass: the main living volume, roughly square footprint, single story plus loft.
- Secondary mass: a smaller stepped-down mass at the rear (kitchen/storage), one full
  story lower, connected by a short covered breezeway rather than butted flush — this is
  the terrain response, not a decorative addition.
- Silhouette: dominant steep gable over the primary mass; the secondary mass's shallower
  shed roof reads clearly as subordinate, giving the roofline one clean break instead of a
  flat run.
- Proportions: wall height kept low relative to roof height — steep-roof-over-low-wall is
  the "mountain house" proportion cue, exaggerated slightly per
  references/proportions.md.
- Palette: stone foundation/chimney, horizontal log primary wall, dark wood-shingle roof,
  minimal accent (iron door hardware only).

## Taste review (example, assumes a profile favoring layered rooflines and controlled asymmetry)
Strong alignment: the roofline break and stepped massing both come from the established
Soft Preference for layered rooflines.
Intentional deviation: no tower/attachment beyond the breezeway, and only three
materials — a small dwelling doesn't carry the detail density or attachment frequency
that preference profile shows on larger civic structures; adding it here would be taste
overfitting (see references/design-principles.md).

## Why this isn't "box + roof"
The rear mass exists because of the slope, not for variety's sake; the roof break follows
from that massing decision instead of being an applied roof gimmick on a single box.
